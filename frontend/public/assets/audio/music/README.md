Volume should be mixed low (~-12 dB) so they sit under SFX without drowning them.

## Tips for sourcing
- Freesound.org — search "cozy ambient loop" (CC0/CC-BY license)
- Pixabay Music — free commercial use
- Zapsplat.com — "nature ambient loop"
- itch.io — search "free music pack cozy RPG"

Compress to 128 kbps MP3 to keep file sizes small.
